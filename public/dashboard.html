If you forget your password, you must remove the extension, clear its local data, and reinstall. Your rules, settings, and browsing insights will be lost. Export anything you want to keep first.

A gentle deterrent, not an unbreakable lock. It cannot stop you disabling or uninstalling the extension, editing its code, or clearing browser data. Unlocking lasts five minutes or until you lock settings. Unusual URL encodings may be checked only after navigation; this is not a firewall or download blocker.
